Damaris Zuluaga

Hi, my name is Damaris, and I am a graphic designer and illustrator with over 30 years of experience in both traditional and digital illustration. Throughout my creative journey, I have developed a passion for visual storytelling and creating designs that communicate ideas clearly and effectively. My work combines artistic creativity with thoughtful design to produce engaging visual solutions. In my free time, I enjoy sketching and experimenting with new illustration ideas. I also like exploring my hometown of Long Beach, where I often take photographs that inspire my creative work. Observing everyday scenes, textures, and colors helps influence my illustrations and design approach.
